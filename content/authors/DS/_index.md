Bio: 
  - Dorenda joined the Shaw lab to study host-parasite interactions that underlie persistence and virulence of Cryptosporidium using single cell approaches.
She completed her PhD in Sylvie Garcia’s lab at the Pasteur Institute in Paris as part of the ParaFrap network. There, she studied the interactions between Plasmodium vivax and the human host in the bone marrow using humanized mice and scRNA-seq.
She is particularly interested in how apicomplexans manipulate their niche and how the host immune system fights back, and curiosity-driven, collaborative research is what drives her. When not in the lab, you can find her playing board games, climbing boulder walls or taking a dance class.
