bio: Sebastian started the Shaw Lab at the Institute of Parasitology at the University of Zurich with an SNSF Starting Grant to study Cryptosporidium, an understudied parasite that affects people and animals worldwide. He completed his PhD in Prof. Isabel Roditi’s group in Bern and his postdoctoral training in Prof. Boris Striepen’s group at the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia. He loves molecular biology and genetics and wants the lab to be a place where people work together, have fun, are curious, and grow into the next generation of biologists. Outside the lab, he enjoys spending time with his family and hiking.
